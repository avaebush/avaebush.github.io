Introduction

I am an Earth systems scientist investigating how climate change influences landscapes, ecosystems, and natural hazards. My research combines geospatial analysis, statistical modeling, and community-engaged science to develop evidence-based strategies for climate adaptation and resource governance. As an educator, I support solutions-driven learning that connects science to environmental challenges through data analysis, quantitative reasoning, and collaborative inquiry.

Research

My research integrates artificial intelligence, climate science, and environmental data analysis to understand how Earth's systems respond to natural and human-driven change. I connect research with experiential learning by facilitating engagement with environmental data, landscape processes, and evidence-based decision making. Current research and education themes include:

Climate Variability, Land Use & Landslide Hazard

My doctoral research examined how precipitation variability, wildfire, and human modification of landscapes influence precipitation-triggered landslide hazard across the western United States. This work required integration of spatially distributed environmental datasets with quantitative statistical approaches to identify relationships between climate forcing, landscape conditions, and Earth-surface response.

Stream Sediment & Carbon Transport

Early research through the River Basins Research Initiative examined particulate organic carbon transport in urban headwater streams, including relationships among stream discharge, sediment load, and carbon dynamics. This work established an early research interest in coupled hydrologic, sediment, and biogeochemical processes within river systems.

Landscape Evolution & Earth Materials

Research spanning geomorphology, geochemistry, tectonics, and environmental Earth science has included field-based investigation of landscape evolution, sedimentology, geological mapping, isotopic analysis, and characterization of Earth materials. These experiences provide a process-based foundation for investigating sediment production and transfer across connected landscapes.
